# 行为准则

[English](#code-of-conduct)

## 我们的承诺

项目社区应提供开放、友善、尊重且不受骚扰的协作环境，不因年龄、外貌、残障、族裔、性别认同与表达、经验水平、国籍、种族、宗教或性取向而区别对待任何人。

## 预期行为

- 尊重不同观点、经验和背景。
- 以具体、建设性的方式提出和接受反馈。
- 讨论聚焦于问题本身，承认错误并修复影响。
- 优先考虑项目和社区的整体利益。

## 不可接受的行为

- 侮辱、贬损、骚扰、威胁或人身攻击。
- 发布他人的私人信息，或未经许可公开他人的私密通信。
- 性骚扰、性暗示语言或不受欢迎的接触。
- 蓄意破坏讨论、误导他人或扰乱协作。
- 其他在专业协作环境中明显不当的行为。

## 适用范围

本准则适用于项目仓库、问题讨论、代码审查，以及代表项目进行的其他公共或私密交流。

## 报告与执行

违规行为应通过维护者提供的私密渠道报告。维护者应对报告进行公平、审慎的调查，尊重相关人员的隐私，并根据行为的性质和影响采取适当措施。

当前维护者联系方式和具体执行流程仍需在公开发布前补充。在这些信息补齐前，本准则的执行渠道尚不完整。

# Code of Conduct

[中文](#行为准则)

## Our Commitment

The project community should provide an open, friendly, respectful, and harassment-free collaboration environment. No one should be treated differently because of age, appearance, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Respect differing viewpoints, experiences, and backgrounds.
- Give and receive feedback in a specific, constructive manner.
- Focus discussions on the issue, acknowledge mistakes, and repair their impact.
- Prioritize the interests of the project and community as a whole.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Insults, derogatory comments, harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information or private communications without permission.
- Sexual harassment, sexualized language, or unwelcome advances.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, misleading others, or obstructing collaboration.
- Other conduct that is clearly inappropriate in a professional collaboration environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the project repository, issue discussions, code reviews, and other public or private communications conducted on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Violations should be reported through a private channel provided by the maintainers. Maintainers should investigate reports fairly and carefully, respect the privacy of everyone involved, and take appropriate action based on the nature and impact of the behavior.

Maintainer contact details and specific enforcement procedures must still be added before public release. Until then, the enforcement channel for this Code of Conduct remains incomplete.
